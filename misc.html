Chair Empire

2026 | Made in Blender & Weebly

I worked with my brother, Calvin, to make Chair Empire. It is a website that is a fake online store selling impractical chairs. The conventional design of Chair Empire’s layout implies there is a market for useless chairs, and a market for useless chairs implies that people are spending money on them. Essentially, Chair Empire is a critique of poor financial decisions regarding ugly luxury items.

Metric Tea and Cereal

2026 | Made with phone camera

A montage of making a cup of earl grey tea and a bowl of cereal. This video uses metric montage, a type of Soviet Montage Theory.
In a metric montage, each cut is based on specific intervals. In this video, each clip lasts 3 seconds before cutting.
