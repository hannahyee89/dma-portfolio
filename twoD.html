Seeing is Bee Leaving

2025 | Made in Photoshop, Audacity, TextEdit

​In a glitch collage, I have depicted a glitched surrounding and a normal-looking bee. The environment juxtaposes the quality of the bee, placing focus on it. Here, glitch alludes to a decaying environment. Bees are very important to the ecosystem and agriculture, and I feel like not enough people are aware of this. In this glitch art, the bee is seen as the only intact form.

Fish Out of Water

2026 | Made in After Effects

A fish...in outer space and a garden? That is most certainly a fish out of water! In this video project, I have made two separate, yet connected gifs. As the fish swims out of frame in the first gif, it enters the second gif. The main purpose of this project was to experiment in After Effects.

Planet Flora

2026 | Made in Illustrator

A planet made of flowers! I made a custom flower pattern and mapped it onto a sphere, both in Adobe Illustrator. I was mainly testing Illustrator's 3D features. While this combines 2D and 3D mediums, I consider it more of a 2D work.
