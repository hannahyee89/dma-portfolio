About Me

I'm Hannah, a Digital Media Art student at San José State University. I'm primarily interested in 2D and 3D art, whether that be through video/animation or still image.

I practice DMA because it combines a lot of mediums I’m either interested in or curious about, such as digital video, animation, 3D modeling, and coding. Additionally, it requires me to learn software I need more experience with, like Illustrator or Photoshop. Acquiring new skills opens up a whole realm of possibilities!

When I was taking ART 74, I became increasingly more interested in DMA and its range of mediums. I am excited about making something that captures the multimedia spirit of DMA.
