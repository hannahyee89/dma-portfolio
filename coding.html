Coding Works

Interactive programs/sites made with HTML, CSS, and p5.js

Pet the Cat!

2025 | Made with HTML

​In this HTML Net Art narrative piece, the user goes through a "Choose Your Own Adventure" story, albeit a very short one. The purpose of this work is to convey a small, whimsical story that is both structured and nonsensical. I think interactive art is particularly intriguing since it engages the viewer in a more involved way.

Mostly Normal Drawing Program

2025 | Made with p5.js (via Processing)

This drawing tool is a medium for the user's own input. It features the typical-type brushes, as well as stamps I drew digitally. Art is not barred to a specific group of people, and I enjoy seeing art other people have made, regardless of skill level. This drawing tool emphasizes this sentiment.​ This was an intro to p5.js through the Processing program.

Let's go Fishing!

2026 | Made with p5.js

An interactive environment with sardines and tropical fish wearing rainbow propellor hats. Use the mouse to look around!
